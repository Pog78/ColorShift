How to Play

Use your Arrow Keys, WASD, Swipe, or the D-Pad to slide the white tile.

Group all tiles of the same color together (Pairs for 3x3, Trios for 5x5 and 7x7) to complete a set.

Standard Rules: Tile groups must be arranged in a straight vertical or horizontal line. The white tile must finish exactly in the center of the board to win!

Easy Mode: Groups can be L-shapes, and the white tile can finish anywhere.

Complete all sets to win!

Press R to restart. Tap anywhere to close this menu.
